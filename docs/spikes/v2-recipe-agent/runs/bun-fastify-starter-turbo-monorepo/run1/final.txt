The recipe is at `.gantry/recipe.json`. The dry-run says `ACCEPTED` and all examples pass.

- **Stack:** Fastify backend (Bun and Turbo monorepo, app in `apps/backend`) with Kysely tables. There is no frontend.
- **Routes:** 0, because the repo has no pages.
- **Endpoints:** 2.
  - `GET /` is the health check.
  - `POST /users/email` gets its full path from the `/` and `/users` register prefixes.
- **Tables:** 2, `users` and `userProviders`, taken from the keys of the Kysely `Database` interface.
- **Not expressed:** nothing missing. The Swagger UI plugin serves `/docs` through a plugin option, not a handler registration. I left it out because the recipe can't express it.

Two mount rules also match `fp()`-wrapped plugins, such as `fastify.register(fp(plugins))`, which are not routers. They add no endpoints.
